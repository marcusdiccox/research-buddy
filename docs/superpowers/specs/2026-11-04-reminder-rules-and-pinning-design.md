# Reminder rules, pinning and variety

User requirements: a single Remind on/off control, fixed warnings 1 day, 6 hours, 2 hours, 1 hour and 30 minutes before an event; more varied walking distances/directions with edge avoidance; automatically hide the writing bubble when focus moves to another app/tab unless pinned; expand jokes/lines to reduce repetition.

Scheduling is persistent and per-reminder. Each fixed checkpoint is delivered once, with reservation/retry handling; missed checkpoints coalesce into one current catch-up rather than a burst. Completed/disabled reminders are silent. Date changes reset the schedule; ordinary title edits do not replay delivered alerts. Pip speaks the alert when visible, and Windows notifications remain an available channel. A visible writing/notebook surface also shows the alert without replacing the draft. Pip must be running for its local alerts.

The bubble starts unpinned, hides on native blur, retains its draft and releases Pip's movement pause. A pin icon keeps it visible above other apps; pin state persists. Internal Notes/Reminder/Calendar mode changes keep the shared draft.

Movement keeps the existing slow pace and modest duty cycle while varying actual trip distance/heading, steering away from nearby work-area edges. Expand each theme to at least 30 short original lines and consume a shuffled bag before repeating. Reminder speech has priority over jokes.
