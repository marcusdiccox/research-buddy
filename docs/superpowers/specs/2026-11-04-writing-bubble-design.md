# Pip writing bubble

User correction: primary click should open an editable speech bubble near Pip, not the regular compact app. Use a frameless transparent360x340 native window with a rounded note surface and a small tail pointing toward the fox. Notes, Reminder and Calendar modes share the current text. Save uses the existing SQLite APIs; a separate Open notebook action expands the current saved note/reminder into the existing notepad. Closing hides the bubble and preserves its draft. Keep Pip stationary while its writing bubble is open.

Settings become a separate frameless340x400 panel: short labels, simple controls, no introduction or explanatory paragraphs. Existing detailed research settings remain accessible in the larger research tools.

Popover placement uses the fox's current display work area, supports negative coordinates, clamps horizontal placement and flips the tail above/below when needed. Google connection functionality and the pending registration/consent handoff are unchanged.
