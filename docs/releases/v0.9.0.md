Pip now has **Fox, Red panda and Otter** companions.

- Separate **Animal** and **Appearance** settings. Natural, Arctic and Midnight colors work with every animal; switching animals keeps your color.
- New generated walking, blinking, playful, sleeping and sit/wake transition sheets. The red panda hugs its ringed tail; the otter rubs its paws and cheeks.
- The otter sometimes swims with a slow paddling motion and transitions into/out of it. Right-click for Tail hug, Float a little or Say hello. Hover pause, monitor bounds and reduced motion still apply.
- Automatic updates from this repository's stable GitHub releases. Download quietly, install on ordinary quit, defer during Windows shutdown. A small refresh button in Settings checks manually.

**Install Pip-Setup-0.9.0.exe once to get automatic updates.** Earlier installers cannot update themselves. Thereafter, published versioned releases include the installer, blockmap and latest.yml together. Regular source commits and prereleases are not installed.

Notes/reminders and automatic local notebook backups remain included. Windows x64 installer is unsigned; Google Calendar setup remains separate. Backups and updates do not upload your notes. Automatic updates run only in installed Windows builds.
